## Motivation and requirements

## Proposed design

Describe boundaries, interfaces, persistent formats or experimental procedure. Identify changes to existing behavior and to contributor responsibilities.

## Alternatives and trade-offs

Compare viable alternatives, including the present design, using evidence. Explain cost, maintainability, compatibility and validity/security consequences.

## Validation

Map acceptance criteria to checks and artifact paths. Include boundary/failure cases, baseline comparisons and recovery verification where relevant.

## Rollout or execution

Describe sequencing, dependencies, resource budget, migration, rollback and stopping conditions. Name the owner of each consequential decision. External release or publication authorization must be explicit.

## Open questions and review

List uncertainties that could change the design, the evidence needed to resolve them, independent challenges and their disposition. Record the plan review through the CLI before claiming a major task. Revise and review again when assumptions or scope change materially.
