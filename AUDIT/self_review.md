# Phase 2 internal critical review

1. **Is it useful for long-term projects?** Durable decisions, task ownership, hypotheses and evidence snapshots allow recovery after chat loss. Full-state histories and manual provider dispatch limit scale; history performance needs Phase3 measurements.
2. **Can a completely new agent understand it?** A central workflow, formal startup protocol, issued Understanding Report, preserved failures and a structured handover give a file-based entry. The fresh-process export/rebuild scenario reconstructs status, decisions and unfinished work. Acknowledgement cannot prove comprehension or external-resource availability.
3. **Can different agents collaborate?** Declared role selection, leases, dependency gates and actual separate-model analysis/challenge/vote records support cooperative work. Capabilities and labels are declarations; adapters do not launch products or authenticate participants. Scripted fixture labels remain distinguished from actual independent reviewers.
4. **Are decisions traceable?** Hash-chained events retain CR alternatives, individual analyses, objections and ballots, exact H/E/R provenance and rejected proposals. Some early Phase2 evidence paths were overwritten before snapshots, so original hashes are reported as historical loss; traceability must not be confused with preserved original bytes or authenticity.
5. **Is it an enterprise system or only documentation?** It has executable state transitions, stale-input rejection, ownership/independence gates, three debate rounds, change control, research records, audit diagnostics, raw experiment execution and recovery. It remains a local framework requiring external identity, provider dispatch, deployment and distributed operations for enterprise use.
6. **What weaknesses remain?** Local trust, editor writes outside leases, no external hash anchor, increasing event-history cost, manual capabilities and source access, bounded novelty assessment, synthetic research and unexecuted hosted-platform/provider checks. Current consistency and historical integrity have separate audit fields; warnings are disclosed rather than hidden.
