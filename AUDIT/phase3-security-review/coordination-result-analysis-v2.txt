Remaining blocking malformed-token case

The revision correctly converts an oversized TTL integer into `InvalidLeaseError` and rejects an unhashable resource before dictionary access. However, `_complete()` still calls `float(token.issued_at)` and `float(token.expires_at)` directly inside its validation expression. An externally constructed token with a huge integer timestamp therefore raises a raw `OverflowError`:

`validate(LeaseToken("r", "h", 1, 10**10000, 2, "n", "uados.memory.v1")) -> OverflowError: int too large to convert to float`

Evidence: `AUDIT/phase3-security-review/coordination-result-negative-probes-v2.json`, SHA-256 `9f2865a8c952fa6f26a58c58f7ccbfbb9baae64babb34d26519006622eb8e32d`. This remains a malformed-input failure-closure gap against the plan's bounded token contract. Catch `OverflowError` around both timestamp conversions (or validate through a bounded finite-number helper) and add a regression test for huge issued and expiry integers. Do not rely on the token dataclass constructor as an authorization boundary: future adapters or callers may deserialize or construct it directly.

Result disposition

Request this narrow validation fix, then rerun focused/full suites, scaffold, benchmark no-mutation and audit with new revision-bound evidence. Preserve the explicit process-local and unauthenticated limits in the result record. The SQLite read-only hardening itself is supported by the independent byte-invariance probe.
